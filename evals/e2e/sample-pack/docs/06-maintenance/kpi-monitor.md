# Stage 6 — Maintenance: KPI monitor and feedback loop

## KPI monitoring
- Weekly active workspaces tracked against the forty-workspace first-quarter goal.
- Task-create p95 latency tracked against the 400ms ceiling.
- Slack notification success rate tracked against the 99% target.

## Cost vs value
- Infrastructure spend tracked against the USD 300 per month ceiling; alert on
  breach.

## Feedback loop
- In-product feedback and support requests are triaged weekly; recurring
  friction becomes a new goal-queue candidate for the next planning round.

## GATE-6 exit
KPIs are reported against the Stage-1 baselines, the cost-to-value balance is
positive, and user feedback is feeding the next planning cycle.
