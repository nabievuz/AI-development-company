# Privacy Policy — Acme SaaS Platform (Draft)

## Data We Collect
We collect account information, usage logs, and payment details necessary to
provide and improve our services.

## Legal Basis for Processing
We process your personal data based on your consent and on the necessity of
processing to perform our contract with you.

## Your Right to Access
You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you at any time by
contacting support@acme.example.

## Withdrawing Consent
Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any
time by adjusting your account settings or contacting support.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time and will notify users of any
material changes by posting a notice on our website.
